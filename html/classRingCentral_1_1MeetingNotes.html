Detailed Description

This objects denotes availability of AI generated meeting notes of certain type as post meeting artefact. The note's content can be accessed via POST '/rcvideo/v1/history/meetings/{meetingId}/access-materials' or POST '/rcvideo/v1/history/links' APIs.
