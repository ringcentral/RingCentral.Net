Synchronizes the list of message threads. Returns the thread records that the current user can monitor (assigned to current user, assigned to other users or unassigned). 

In {\ttfamily FSync} mode, only {\ttfamily Open} and {\ttfamily Alive}threads are returned. The records are sorted by {\ttfamily last\+Modified\+Time} in descending order.
